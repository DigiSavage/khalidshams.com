For the final exercise in “2036, built in the open,” give someone else the design.

Include the permitted outcome, source versions, tool contracts, acceptance cases, cost assumptions, stop conditions and named owner.

Ask them to reproduce one allowed result, one justified refusal and one recovery path. Record every question they have to ask you. Those questions show where the design still depends on its author.

The teaching studio exports a design record you can use as a starting point. It is a synthetic example, not production certification:
<https://khalidshams.com/architect/agent-studio/#record>

2036 remains the design horizon. Today's proof is reproducibility.

Agents may write more of the implementation. I still want a person who can answer for the outcome.
