“Future-proof” is easy to write in a proposal.

Try changing one assumption.

The preferred model becomes unavailable. The source refresh slows down. A workflow moves from preparing a draft to sending it.

Choose one. Work backward from the accepted outcome. Find the first dependency that can no longer support it, then trace which permissions, tests, costs and owners must change.

For my 2036 design horizon, I want that reasoning recorded. A replaceable adapter still needs a fresh behavioral evaluation and an approved data boundary. New authority needs new acceptance evidence.

The exercise ends when another engineer can follow the change without guessing.
