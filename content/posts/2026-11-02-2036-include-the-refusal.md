A demo that succeeds every time may have an excellent editor.

For the next part of my 2036 design exercise, I want the uncut version.

Give the system three synthetic cases: enough evidence, missing evidence, and evidence it cannot access. Agree on the expected result before running them.

Inspect the final business state. Did it complete the allowed task, ask for what was missing, and leave the forbidden record untouched? Repeat the cases after a model or prompt change.

Count a justified refusal as the expected outcome. Record an unsupported answer as a failure, however beautifully written.
