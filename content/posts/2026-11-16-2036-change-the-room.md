Try explaining the same proposed system to the sponsor, the security reviewer and the person who will operate it.

The sponsor needs the outcome and trade-off. Security needs the authority boundary. Operations needs the exception path and owner.

For my 2036 design exercise, all three explanations must resolve to the same decision record. Change the audience, then ask each person what the system is permitted to do.

If their answers conflict, the presentation has hidden an architectural problem.
