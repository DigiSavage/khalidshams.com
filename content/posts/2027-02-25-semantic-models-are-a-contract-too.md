If three reports define revenue three ways, you don't have a data platform. You have three opinions with charts attached.

I've seen the argument play out in leadership meetings. Finance has one number, sales has another, the operations dashboard has a third. Each is correct by its own definition. None of the definitions are written down anywhere a human can find them.

The semantic model is where that gets fixed, and it's the layer most teams under-invest in. A governed semantic model in Fabric is a contract: this is what revenue means, this is how it's calculated, this is the grain, this is the owner. Every report reads from it. Nobody redefines it in a measure on a Friday afternoon.

Getting there is more organisational than technical. Someone has to own each definition. Someone has to say no to the report that wants a slightly different one. The platform makes the rule enforceable; it doesn't make the rule.

Once it exists, though, something changes. Arguments about what the number is become arguments about what to do about it. That's the conversation leadership should be having.

How many definitions of your most important metric exist right now?
