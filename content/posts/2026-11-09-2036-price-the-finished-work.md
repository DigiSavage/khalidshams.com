The token bill is only one receipt.

For my 2036 design exercise, I would price a completed unit of work from intake to acceptance. Include model calls, retrieval, retries, infrastructure and the person checking the result.

Try this with a small, clearly labeled test batch. Keep the same tasks and acceptance criteria for the manual baseline and automated version. Record failures and unfinished cases alongside accepted ones.

Divide the total delivery cost by accepted outcomes. If nothing is accepted, report that. A zero in the cost column would be very creative accounting.

Then ask whether the quality, elapsed time and cost justify the complexity.
