## How one episode should work

Start with the method and choose the audience and decision. Use the research desk for dated evidence, including its population and limitations. Research priorities reflect consequence and teachability; they do not establish ticket prices, consulting demand or guaranteed income.

Create an episode explicitly. In **The decision**, name the task, owner and observable outcome. In **Shooting plan**, connect the opening discovery question to an architectural consequence, a demonstrable behavior and an artifact to keep. Preserve the spoken words, exact clicks, visual direction and division of work in each beat. The plan should let Khalid focus on explaining a decision instead of finding the next control during recording.

In **Evidence**, check sources and distinguish proposed, simulated and observed results. In **Get ready**, rehearse the actual screen sequence, inspect the frame and audio, and check the saved artifact. Use the rehearsal view to test timing and flow. Keep recorded words in **Transcript**, separately from the draft script. Carry the verified artifact and unanswered question into the next episode.

The eight-part first season is connected planning. Only build/buy/process, exact approval and the first Foundry checkpoint have linked authored shooting drafts. All remaining entries are planned, and every episode still requires its own evidence and rehearsal. Draft status never means recorded, deployed or ready to publish.

For cross-channel work, retain the same argument and evidence in an optional LinkedIn observation and free site companion. `docs/site/LINKEDIN_2036_SERIES.md` is authoritative for actual scheduling: the base Tuesday/Thursday cadence and the bounded five-post week through November 20. Do not duplicate its calendar in the runtime playbook or change schedules as part of video preparation.

## Preservation and limits

The committed playbook is durable source-controlled direction. It must not be silently replaced by whatever appeared in the latest prompt. Update it when Khalid changes a commitment, preserve the earlier decision in Git history and increment its version. Existing private drafts should be reviewed against updated guidance rather than silently rewritten.

Private episodes retain the existing 365-day lifetime from creation; edits do not extend it. Downloads provide deliberate device copies of shooting packs, draft scripts and supplied recorded transcripts. Do not promise permanent private storage or an automatic backup mechanism that has not been implemented. The studio does not automatically record, transcribe, deploy, schedule or publish.

The template content and playbook are authored source in a public repository. Keep private client details, credentials, actual owner transcripts and unpublished personal production records out of these files. Runtime authorization, session clearing and private exports continue to use the existing Video Studio safeguards.
