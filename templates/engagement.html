{{SCENARIO_TITLE}}

This is a solution I designed to make my approach inspectable. It is not a historical client engagement, a live deployment or a claim of measured savings.

The business question: can the team prepare a reliable quote with fewer repeated handoffs, while keeping pricing and dispatch authority with the people responsible?

D-01 · Proposed pilot boundary

Prepare a quote draft from permitted, current source records. A named, authorized person approves the exact draft. The pilot ends there. Sending and dispatch stay in the existing authorized workflow.

Success evidence
Compare accepted drafts, rework, elapsed time and total handling effort against an observed baseline.
Observed results
Not measured. Baselines and thresholds must be agreed with the actual process owner.
Read the first meeting agenda
  1. Before the call: confirm participants, decision rights, business model, current tools and the question the meeting must resolve. Send a short pre-read. Mark hypotheses as hypotheses.
  2. First 10 minutes: ask the sponsor what decision is needed, then ask the operator to walk one ordinary request and one exception.
  3. Next 20 minutes: map handoffs, source records, permission boundaries and the baseline. Invite disagreement from security and delivery.
  4. Next 15 minutes: compare process improvement, deterministic automation and a bounded AI-assisted pilot. Demonstrate only the uncertain part, with approved or synthetic data and a failure path.
  5. Final 15 minutes: read back the decision, exclusions, unknowns, owners and next evidence request. Issue a decision note for correction before treating it as agreed.

An example 60-minute agenda, adapted to the people and decision in the room.

    04 · Architecture choices

    The platform follows the operating model.

    My first alternative is process improvement or ordinary automation. I add AI where evidence shows it improves an uncertain task. Existing ERP, service management and automation platforms retain their responsibilities when they already do the job well.

    01

    Intake and identity

    Approved mailbox or form. Preserve the original request and its source ID. Authenticate the user; establish customer, role and purpose before retrieving business records.

    Azure foundation: separate environments, network boundaries, workload identities, secrets, infrastructure as code and cost owners.

    02

    Operational evidence

    The ERP remains authoritative for customer, asset, pricing and work-order state. Read through scoped APIs and check freshness. Missing ownership, stale prices or denied access stop the draft.

    Fabric can support governed analytical copies. An analytical snapshot is not authority to commit a live price.

    03

    Prepare and validate

    Extract the requested work and draft a quote with source references. Deterministic rules validate required fields, pricing and policy. Evaluate useful drafts, unsupported claims, refusals and failure cases.

    Copilot Studio, Microsoft Foundry or conventional services are alternatives to assess, not a mandatory bundle.

    04

    Human decision

    A reviewer sees the original request, current facts, uncertainty and proposed quote together. Rejection records a reason. Approval is bound to the exact version and does not grant general write authority.

    The worked pilot ends at an approved draft. Sending and dispatch remain in the existing authorized workflow.

    Separate measurement path: approved source events → Data Factory ingestion → governed OneLake data → semantic model and operational reporting. Reconcile missing and duplicate events before measuring outcomes.

    Process and ordinary automation

    Start here when fields, rules and routing are stable. Improve the intake form, deterministic validation and exception queue. Avoid a model when a simpler change meets the outcome.

    Decision evidence: compare manual effort and failure rates on the same representative tasks before paying for additional complexity.

    Copilot Studio

    Assess graphical authoring, the required channels and connectors, the maker team's skills and how the customer will govern and support the workflow. It has administration, evaluation and governance capabilities.

    Verify: the exact connector actions, authentication, environment policies, release process and commercial terms. Official reference.

    Microsoft Foundry

    Assess model and runtime requirements, integration, evaluations, networking and the Azure operating model. Foundry supports declarative and code-based approaches; product choice alone does not make a system production-ready.

    Verify: capability status, supported regions, model lifecycle, quota, processing boundaries and ownership. Official reference.

    Fabric decisions I resolve before promising the data layer

    Sources and ingestion

    Inventory sources and owners. Prove each connector's supported operation, authentication, gateway or network needs, refresh cadence, schema changes and replay behavior. Data Factory surfaces differ; an available connector does not guarantee the required action.

    Connector reference →

    Workspaces and access

    Separate makers, operators and consumers. Workspace management permissions and granular data access serve different purposes. Admin, Member and Contributor roles have broad access; do not assign those roles to consumers and expect row filters to restrict them. Test each actual query path.

    OneLake security reference →

    Capacity and licensing

    Measure ingestion overlap, query concurrency, peak load, throttling and recovery. Capacity and per-user licensing are different inputs. PPU alone does not provision capacity for non-Power-BI workloads. Check creator and viewer needs before choosing a capacity and commercial commitment.

    Capacity and licensing reference →

    Value and operating cost

    Record baseline, target, observed result, measurement window and evidence source separately. Include cloud, licenses, model usage, retries, human review and support in cost per accepted outcome. Leave observed results empty until there is real evidence.
