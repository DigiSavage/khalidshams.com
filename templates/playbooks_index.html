How to use these

Read the questions first. If your team can answer them in writing, skip to the build.

The questions are the assessment. The build is the plan. The failure section is the review. If you are a leader deciding whether a programme is ready, the questions alone will tell you. If you are an engineer about to deploy, the build order is the part that saves you a quarter. If you are interviewing me, this is the process you would be hiring.

Same shape, every time

Questions, build, why, use cases, failures, deliverables. Once you know the shape you can read any of them in ten minutes and find the part you need.

Azure named, not required

The tools are Azure because that is where most of this was delivered. The order is architecture, not vendor. One of the systems described here runs on AWS and one on Anthropic's platform, and the sequence was identical.

Every playbook ends at the gates

Each one maps its failure modes to the eight gates of the SHAMS Method, so a finding in any playbook has a sign-off it has to pass.
