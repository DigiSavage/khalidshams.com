How measurements are defined

Observed visitors are the union of consenting random browser identifiers over the selected period, not the sum of daily unique counts and not exact people. Cleared storage, multiple devices, blocked scripts, declined consent and incomplete bot filtering affect counts.

Sessions restart after 30 minutes without observed activity. Average active time uses measured page instances. Time accrues while visible and within 30 seconds of activity, in updates capped at 15 seconds. Simultaneous tabs can overlap: totals are page engagement, not deduplicated human attention.

Active in the last 5 minutes means sessions with a positive engagement update received for an event in that window. Exits are the last observed page in the selected period. Missing measurements are unavailable. LCP is a browser rendering measurement, not server availability.
