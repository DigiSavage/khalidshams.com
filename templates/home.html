Agentic AI

A complete agentic system, zero to delivered

Context
A client needed an agentic AI system built on Anthropic's Claude platform. Not a proof of concept, a delivered system.
What I did
Owned every milestone from inception through completed delivery: solution architecture and agent boundary design, multi-agent orchestration, tool and function integration against real systems of record, an evaluation harness with a written failure-mode catalogue, and production hardening: guardrails, fallback paths, observability, cost controls.
Outcome
Delivered and hardened. Comparative evaluation across Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and DeepSeek informed the model and orchestration choices rather than assumption.

Multi-tenant SaaS

1,000+ tenant databases, stabilised

Context
An ISV running a SaaS estate on 1,000+ Azure SQL tenant databases with unreproducible performance complaints and unpredictable cost.
What I did
Classified workloads, defined explicit Business Critical graduation criteria, worked the noisy-neighbour and CPU risk directly, and laid a staged path toward Fabric and AI readiness on a foundation that could carry it.
Outcome
Tier decisions became rule-based instead of complaint-driven, and the modernisation path stopped being blocked by platform instability.

Business value

$4.5M in cost avoidance across ~3,000 employees

Context
An organisation of roughly 3,000 employees and 10 vendors, with overlapping tooling and untested assumptions about its own resilience.
What I did
Led a business-value assessment mapping applications, data, infrastructure, identity, security, cost and dependencies onto a single view, documenting 32 gaps and connecting each to a cloud or services decision.
Outcome
$4.5M in identified cost avoidance. Almost none of it from renegotiation, nearly all of it from finding what the organisation was doing twice.

Regulated

Data, security and governance for a financial services programme

Context
A multi-million-dollar initiative across banking and capital markets stakeholders, with regulatory obligations shaping every architectural option.
What I did

Outcome
A governed foundation the programme could build data and AI capability on without relitigating compliance at every step.
